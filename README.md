# Leonida Legend — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

An offline guide and tracker for all **55 Grand Theft Auto VI PlayStation 5 trophies** (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden), built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/gta6-trophy-guide-01) bounty `gta6-trophy-guide-01`.

Open `app/index.html` in a browser — no build step, no network. Tick trophies off as you earn them; progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.

## What it covers

- **Every trophy** with its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them, plus a guide in our own words: what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed.
- **Filters** by kind (Story, Missable, Collectibles, Activities) and by grade, plus search.
- **Spoiler setting** that hides the names and descriptions of hidden story trophies until you earn them.
- **A five-stage roadmap to the platinum:**
  1. **Story first** — Gone Fishing, Chapters 1–4, the final mission, and the named-mission trophies along the way.
  2. **Contacts and strangers** — final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus 8 stranger strands.
  3. **Activities and skills** — Shooting Range gold (42 challenges), 250 stunts, races, drone races, fishing, arcade, combat.
  4. **Collectibles and exploration** — cards, WAiNK It locations (100), vehicles (100), areas of Leonida (52), meditation spots (18), garages (8), Scooter Bros registrations (10).
  5. **Money and clean-up** — launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000, reach 100% completion, platinum.
- **A missables page** listing every trophy tied to a specific story beat, a contact's final mission, a partner moment, or a one-off scene — with the stage to earn it in.

## How the guides cite sources

Official names, descriptions and grades come from Exophase's official list — Exophase, *Grand Theft Auto VI trophies*, https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/ — with PSN counts where PSNProfiles lists one — PSNProfiles, *Grand Theft Auto VI trophies*, https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi. Every trophy's guide names its sources beside it in the app's detail pane. Where a step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location; that advice is labelled "our own advice from the official description, not yet checked in the game". Hidden flags follow PSNProfiles' list as published before this bounty opened. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # serves app/ at http://localhost:8080
```

Or hand it in exactly as the kit describes:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
